note: quetz runs freestanding firmware (or your app + drivers ported onto the
quetz startup scaffold). Unmodified board BSPs are NOT supported yet: QEMU's
mcf5208evb reads unmodeled SoC registers as zero and ignores writes, so BSP
init HANGS on status polls (PLL lock etc.) and silently loses config writes —
it does not crash. Porting pattern and details:
sst-elements/src/sst/elements/quetz/SIMULATING-YOUR-SYSTEM.md
